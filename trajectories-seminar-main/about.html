Trajectories is convened by two early career researchers working on how social experiences shape political behaviour.

CW

Clara Weißenfels

University of Siegen

Clara is a postdoctoral researcher at the Chair for International Political Economy, Department of Social Sciences, University of Siegen. Her research examines political socialisation and how experiences in different social contexts shape political attitudes and behaviour.

Website

GM

Giacomo Melli

University of Oxford

Giacomo holds a DPhil in Sociology from the University of Oxford. His research sits between social stratification and political sociology, with a focus on intergenerational class transmission, electoral participation, and political inequality. His work has appeared in the British Journal of Sociology, Research in Social Stratification and Mobility, and the Journal of European Public Policy.

Website

Present with us

We would be happy to hear from researchers working on political socialisation, life course dynamics, inequality, or related questions of how social experiences shape political orientations and behaviour. Work in progress is welcome.
